equally at every size. The L2 plateau is the reason a two-pass formulation
of the operator is worth considering: a token tile whose sources stay

### Profiling constraints

**Nsight Compute cannot be used on this host.** `ncu` is installed and
runs, but collecting hardware counters fails with `ERR_NVGPUCTRPERM`:
the driver restricts performance counters to administrators
(`NVreg_RestrictProfilingToAdminUsers`). Changing that is a host-wide
modification, so this project does not do it, and no claim here rests on
an `ncu` counter.

The substitutes used instead:

- **Nsight Systems** (`nsys`) works without special privileges, and gives
  kernel timelines, launch counts and durations.
- **`torch.profiler`** gives per-kernel CUDA time and launch counts.
- **Analytical roofline.** This operator is memory-bound by roughly two
  orders of magnitude, so achieved bandwidth derived from measured
  latency and an exact byte count answers most of what `ncu` would.
